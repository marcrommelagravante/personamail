# PersonaMail — Landing Page Copy (Final)

Brand voice reference: relationship-aware communication. Avoid "AI Generator / AI Rewrite / AI Grammar / Magic Prompt / AI Magic / Powered by AI." No robot avatars, no prompt-box-as-default-interface. Use the existing product language: **Compose · Contacts · Improve · Review · Communication Style.**

## 2. Problem / Value Proposition

**Section Headline:**
Every relationship deserves its own voice.

**Body Copy:**
The way you write to your manager isn't the way you write to a close colleague — and it shouldn't be.

Most email tools give you one generic voice for everyone. PersonaMail remembers who you're writing to, so your tone, warmth, and formality shift naturally with the relationship.

**Supporting Statement:**
Every conversation remembers who it's for.

**Design Recommendation:**
A simple before/after comparison — "One generic voice" vs. "A voice that adapts to the relationship" — makes the core difference clear at a glance.

---

## 3. Features

**Section Headline:**
Everything you need to write with confidence.

Four feature cards:

### Compose
**Start with the idea. PersonaMail helps shape it into the right message.**
Start from a blank page and create a complete draft in a tone that fits — formal, friendly, or casual — tailored to the person you're writing to.

### Contacts
**Remember the person, not just the email address.**
Build a communication profile for each person you email, including their preferred tone, your history, and the details that make every message feel personal instead of generic.

### Improve
**Already wrote it? Make it better without losing your voice.**
Improve refines your draft by sharpening the tone, tightening the wording, and helping the message land the way you intended.

### Review
**Make sure your message is ready before you send it.**
Review catches grammar and clarity issues so every email goes out polished, clear, and ready.

---

## 4. How It Works

**Section Headline:**
Three steps to a better email.

**Step 1 — Add a contact**
Tell PersonaMail who you're writing to and how you usually communicate with them.

**Step 2 — Compose or Improve**
Write from scratch or bring in a draft. PersonaMail shapes the message to fit the relationship.

**Step 3 — Review and send**
Check the message, make your final adjustments, and send with confidence.

**Visual flow:**
Add a contact → Compose or Improve → Review and send

## Brand Voice Rules

Keep the page: warm, clear, professional, human, confident without being exaggerated.

Avoid: "AI Generator," "AI Rewrite," "AI Grammar," "Magic Prompt," "AI Magic," "Powered by AI," robot avatars, prompt-box-first interfaces.
